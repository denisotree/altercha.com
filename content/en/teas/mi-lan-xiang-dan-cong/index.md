Mi Lan Xiang, or "Honey Orchid", is one of the most celebrated aroma types of Dan Cong oolong from Guangdong province. It is famous for its bright, layered fragrance of ripe fruit, honey and flowers.

## Origin

Feng Huang Shan (Phoenix Mountain), Guangdong province, China. The leaves come from the peaks of the mountain, where the climate and soil are especially favourable for tea.

## Flavor & Aroma Profile

- **Aroma**: Complex and intoxicating, with notes of ripe fruit, honey and sweet flowers.
- **Taste**: Rich and dense, with distinct citrus-honey notes and an expressive astringency.
- **Finish**: Long, with honey and citrus.

## Effects & Benefits

**Tea Superpowers:**
- Energising and uplifting
- Lifts the mood
- One of the most aromatic oolongs in our collection
- Reveals itself in a gaiwan: porcelain brings out the aroma, clay adds depth

## Brewing Instructions

**Simple brewing method:**
- **For 300-500ml teapot**: 4-5g of tea
- **Water temperature**: 90-95°C
- **Steeping time**: 1-2 minutes

Do not oversteep: Dan Cong quickly turns astringent and bitter. In a gaiwan, use 5g per 100ml of boiling water and short steepings.
