GABA Oolong Emerald Green is the freshest of our GABA oolongs. Lightly oxidised and lightly roasted, it stays close to classic green Taiwanese oolongs, while keeping the slight tartness and baked notes typical of GABA teas.

## Origin

Ali Shan high mountain region, Taiwan. Harvested in winter (October–November) 2025 from Qing Xin bushes, rolled into spheres and lightly roasted. The leaves are processed with GABA technology, developed by Japanese specialists in the late 1980s: limiting oxygen during processing raises the natural content of gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA) in the tea.

## Flavor & Aroma Profile

- **Aroma**: Sweet, floral and creamy.
- **Taste**: Fresh and floral, sweet with a pleasant bitterness. Bright and rich.
- **Finish**: Fresh, with a light tartness typical of GABA teas.

## Effects & Benefits

**Tea Superpowers:**
- Refreshing and relaxing at the same time
- Gently helps concentration
- The lightness of a green oolong with the depth of GABA
- High GABA content supports calm and balance

## Brewing Instructions

**Simple brewing method:**
- **For 300-500ml teapot**: 4-6g of tea
- **Water temperature**: 85-90°C
- **Steeping time**: 2-3 minutes

Great for the daytime, when you want to stay calm without losing focus.
