GABA Oolong Ruby Black is a classic Taiwanese GABA oolong with a warm, fruity character. The leaves are rolled into tight spheres and given a medium roast, which brings out notes of berries, dried fruit and fresh bread.

## Origin

Ali Shan, Taiwan. Harvested in 2025 from Qing Xin bushes at an elevation of about 1500 m. The leaves are processed with GABA technology, developed by Japanese specialists in the late 1980s: limiting oxygen during processing raises the natural content of gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA) in the tea.

## Flavor & Aroma Profile

- **Aroma**: Soft and rich, with fruity and berry notes and a hint of bread.
- **Taste**: Soft, juicy and warm, with notes of baked bread and dried fruit.
- **Finish**: Refreshing, with a bright berry tartness.

## Effects & Benefits

**Tea Superpowers:**
- Clearly relaxing and meditative
- Gently uplifting and helps concentration
- Warming medium roast without harshness
- High GABA content supports calm and balance

## Brewing Instructions

**Simple brewing method:**
- **For 300-500ml teapot**: 4-6g of tea
- **Water temperature**: 90-95°C
- **Steeping time**: 2-3 minutes

A good companion for a slow evening or a calm afternoon.
