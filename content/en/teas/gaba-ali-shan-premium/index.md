GABA Oolong Alishan Premium is the softer and deeper sibling of our GABA Alishan Classic. A lower degree of roasting keeps the tea smooth and juicy, with the sweetness of fresh bread and a touch of honey.

## Origin

Ali Shan high mountain region, Taiwan. Harvested in winter (October–November) 2025 from Qing Xin bushes and given a medium roast. The leaves are processed with GABA technology, developed by Japanese specialists in the late 1980s: limiting oxygen during processing raises the natural content of gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA) in the tea.

## Flavor & Aroma Profile

- **Aroma**: Sweet, with baked bread and a touch of honey.
- **Taste**: Soft and juicy, with notes of sweet bread and dried fruit.
- **Finish**: Smooth and sweet.

## Effects & Benefits

**Tea Superpowers:**
- Relaxing and meditative
- Gently sharpens concentration
- Softer and deeper than GABA Alishan Classic
- High GABA content supports calm and balance

## Brewing Instructions

**Simple brewing method:**
- **For 300-500ml teapot**: 4-6g of tea
- **Water temperature**: 90-95°C
- **Steeping time**: 2-3 minutes

A gentle tea for the afternoon and evening, when you want to slow down while staying clear-headed.
