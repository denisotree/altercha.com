Lao Cha Wang 2012 is an aged Taiwanese oolong that has been maturing for over a decade. Time has turned it soft, clean and deep, with notes of caramel, spices, nuts and crème brûlé.

## Origin

Taiwan, harvest of 2012. Lao Cha Wang starts as a regular Taiwanese oolong, roasted to a moisture level suitable for storage. The master keeps a small part of the year's best tea in large stone or clay vessels covered with thick paper that absorbs moisture. Every two to three years the tea is taken out, checked and carefully re-roasted to remove excess moisture and preserve its aroma. After at least three years it develops the softness typical of aged oolongs, and experts consider six to eight years the minimum age of maturity.

## Flavor & Aroma Profile

- **Aroma**: Woody-spicy and nutty, with a menthol tartness.
- **Taste**: Soft, clean and multifaceted: roasted, sweet-caramel, spicy-nutty, with notes of dried roots and crème brûlé.
- **Finish**: Juicy, long-lasting and rich.

## Effects & Benefits

**Tea Superpowers:**
- Harmonising and relaxing
- Meditative, perfect for a mindful tea ceremony
- Over a decade of patient ageing and re-roasting
- Withstands many steepings, the aroma keeps changing

## Brewing Instructions

**Simple brewing method:**
- **For 300-500ml teapot**: 5-7g of tea
- **Water temperature**: 92-97°C
- **Steeping time**: 3-4 minutes

Best of all in a preheated gaiwan or a porous Yixing teapot: 5g per 100ml of water at 92-97°C, many short steepings.
