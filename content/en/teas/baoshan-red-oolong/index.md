Baoshan Red Oolong is a high mountain oolong from Yunnan, made from classic Taiwanese Qing Xin bushes. Thanks to a higher oxidation level it comes close to delicate black teas, with a soft, sweet floral-honey cup.

## Origin

Baoshan region, northwestern Yunnan, China, at an elevation of over 1900 m. Taiwanese Qing Xin bushes were planted here on ecological high mountain plantations, where tea is grown without pesticides or chemicals. The tea is made using red oolong (Hong Shui) technology, like the Taiwanese Hong Shui oolongs. Harvested in winter (November) 2025.

## Flavor & Aroma Profile

- **Aroma**: Caramel and roasted nuts.
- **Taste**: Floral-honey, sweet and soft.
- **Finish**: Warm and sweet.

## Effects & Benefits

**Tea Superpowers:**
- Warming
- Gently relaxing
- Clears the mind
- Taiwanese craftsmanship meets Yunnan mountain terroir

## Brewing Instructions

**Simple brewing method:**
- **For 300-500ml teapot**: 4-6g of tea
- **Water temperature**: 90-95°C
- **Steeping time**: 2-3 minutes

A cosy cup for cold days and quiet evenings.
